        [out] The **CAP theorem** states that a distributed data store cannot simultaneously satisfy three desirable properties:

1.  **Consistency (C)**: Every read receives the most recent write or an error.
2.  **Availability (A)**: Every request receives a (possibly non-cached) response, without guarantee that it contains the most recent write.
3.  **Partition Tolerance (P)**: The system continues to operate despite an arbitrary number of messages being dropped or delayed (network partitions).
